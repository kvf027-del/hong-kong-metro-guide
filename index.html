Hong Kong Metro Guide

A fast metro map and journey planner for Hong Kong, for iPhone.

  • The whole network on one map, with station names in English and Chinese.
  • Routes in an instant, with changes, walking and the fare (Octopus and single ticket).
  • Live minutes to the next trains, and a warning if you may miss the last train.
  • Search in English, Traditional or Simplified Chinese, or pinyin.
  • Small and offline-first: the map, routes and fares work without a connection.

Data

Lines, stations, fares and live train times are open data from DATA.GOV.HK, provided by MTR Corporation Limited; the intellectual property rights in the data belong to the Government of the Hong Kong SAR and MTR Corporation Limited. Ride and change times and first and last trains were gathered from the operator's public journey planner and published timetables and may differ from the actual service. Routes, times and fares are estimates, provided as is.
