Privacy Policy

Hong Kong Metro Guide · effective 13 November 2026

In short

The app does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your light or dark map choice and which drawing of the map you use are kept on your phone only. Stations you search for and routes you plan are never sent anywhere.

Live train times

When you open a station, the app asks the Hong Kong Government's open-data service (rt.data.gov.hk) for that station's next trains. The request contains only the line and station codes. Like any website, that service receives your device's IP address to answer; see the DATA.GOV.HK terms and the Government's privacy policy. The app sends nothing else and adds no identifiers.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that collects data (for example advertising), this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask for your permission where the law or Apple requires it.
