Support

Found a wrong time, fare or station name, or have an idea? Email us at founders@harbourstone.kz and tell us the stations, the time and what you expected. We usually reply within a few working days.

If you have a GitHub account, you can also open an issue.

Common questions

Is this the official MTR app? No. It is an independent app built on open data and is not affiliated with MTR Corporation.

Does it work offline? The map, routes, fares and first and last trains work without a connection. Live minutes to the next train need the internet.

Why does the app say I may miss the last train? At night it compares your route with each line's last train. It never hides a route; it warns. Times may differ on public holidays: check station notices.

Is the fare exact? Fares come from the operator's open data for adults, with Octopus and single journey tickets. Concessions and promotions are not included.
